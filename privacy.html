Privacy Policy

CF Ecommerce Reporting
Last updated: 24 September 2026

What the app is

CF Ecommerce Reporting is a private reporting tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app has read-only access to data in accounts the owner is already authorised to use: Google Analytics reports, Google Search Console performance data and Google Merchant Center product data. It cannot change anything in those accounts.

How the data is used

The data is used only to prepare internal ecommerce performance reports for the owner. It is not sold, rented, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Storage and security

Data is kept only as long as needed to produce reports, in systems controlled by the owner, and access credentials are stored securely.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
